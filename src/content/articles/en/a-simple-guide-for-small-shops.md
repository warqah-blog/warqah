Starting small gives you room to learn quickly. Pick one clear product, understand its buyer and test demand before expanding.

## One clear product

Focus on a specific benefit and describe it in language customers understand immediately.

## Measure what matters

Track visits, orders and repeated questions. Those signals reveal what deserves improvement.
