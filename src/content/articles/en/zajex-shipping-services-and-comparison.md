When customers in Riyadh expect an order today rather than several days from now, the question is not simply **who can carry the parcel?** A better question is: who can connect the order to your store, collect it quickly, keep its status visible and deliver it within the right service window? That is the problem **ZAJEX** aims to solve for ecommerce merchants.

<p><strong>Short answer:</strong> ZAJEX is a last-mile delivery platform built for online stores. It can be a strong option when delivery speed is part of the customer experience, but it is not automatically the right replacement for every traditional carrier or shipment.</p>

## What is ZAJEX?

[ZAJEX describes its service](https://zajex.co/en) as a logistics partner for ecommerce stores. Its core role is last-mile delivery: moving a packed, ready-to-ship order from the merchant to the customer. It is not presented as a warehousing, picking or packing service, so the merchant remains responsible for preparing the parcel and accurate recipient data.

ZAJEX is also listed in the [Salla App Store](https://apps.salla.sa/ar/app/1151632797) as a Riyadh last-mile platform offering fast delivery and live shipment tracking. For Salla merchants, the integration matters because it can reduce repeated manual entry and bring shipment creation and tracking closer to the store's existing workflow.

## ZAJEX services explained

### 1. Bullet delivery in 1–4 hours

Bullet is the fastest ZAJEX option in eligible Riyadh coverage areas. It can suit urgent purchases, gifts, time-sensitive products and stores that want delivery within hours to be a visible checkout advantage.

The 1–4-hour window is not a blanket promise for every address. ZAJEX states that merchants can check whether an order qualifies before confirming the shipment.

### 2. Express delivery within 24 hours

Express is intended for orders that need same-day or within-24-hour delivery without using the shortest Bullet window. It can suit scheduled collections from a merchant's warehouse and distribution throughout the day.

### 3. Shipment tracking and status updates

ZAJEX provides shipment status visibility from collection to delivery. That can reduce “Where is my order?” requests and help the store identify delays or failed delivery attempts sooner.

### 4. Store integrations and automated labels

The ZAJEX homepage currently marks **Salla and OTO** as available integrations, while Zid and Shopify are labelled as coming soon. Elsewhere, its FAQ says Salla, Zid and Shopify are supported. Because these official pages are not fully aligned, merchants should verify the [current integration status](https://zajex.co/en#integration) before building an operational process around it.

### 5. Cash on delivery and returns

ZAJEX materials describe support for cash on delivery, returns and reverse logistics. Collection limits, settlement times and return fees may depend on the merchant agreement, so request those terms in writing before activation.

## When is ZAJEX a good fit?

ZAJEX may be a strong option when:

- A meaningful share of your customers are in Riyadh.
- Delivery speed affects conversion or repeat purchases.
- You use Salla or another currently supported integration and want less manual entry.
- You need clear tracking for urgent orders.
- Your team can prepare parcels quickly enough to benefit from fast collection.

You may still need another carrier when most orders travel outside Riyadh, your products have unusual size or handling requirements, or a lower shipping price matters more than delivery within hours.

> The best strategy may be multi-carrier: use ZAJEX for urgent Riyadh orders and a traditional carrier for other cities or less time-sensitive shipments.

## How to test ZAJEX before a full rollout

Run a controlled pilot instead of moving every order at once:

1. Select 20–30 similar orders within a clearly eligible area.
2. Measure collection time, first-attempt delivery rate and tracking accuracy.
3. Calculate the full cost, including COD, returns and failed deliveries.
4. Ask customers whether updates were clear and arrival times met expectations.
5. Compare the results with your current carrier using the same scorecard.

Do not judge the service only by average speed. Track the worst cases too, because a late order can affect customer trust more than an on-time order improves it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does ZAJEX charge a monthly subscription?

The official FAQ says there is no monthly subscription and merchants pay for actual shipments. The website does not publish a universal rate card, so request a quote that covers delivery, returns and COD.

### Does ZAJEX deliver outside Riyadh?

ZAJEX says it delivers to cities across Saudi Arabia and that shipments outside Riyadh typically take 1–4 business days depending on destination and operating conditions. The 1–4-hour Bullet service is limited to Riyadh.

### Can I connect ZAJEX to Salla?

Yes. ZAJEX advertises a Salla integration and is listed in the Salla App Store. Check the required permissions and account settings before launch.

### Is ZAJEX better than my current carrier?

That depends on customer locations, order volume, required speed, total cost and delivery success rate. A small live pilot is more useful than choosing solely from marketing claims.

## The bottom line

ZAJEX's clearest value is turning eligible Riyadh delivery from a multi-day wait into a service measured in hours, supported by ecommerce-focused tracking and integrations. If that solves a real problem for your store, run a limited pilot and request written terms for pricing, coverage, COD and returns. If nationwide reach or the lowest possible cost is the priority, a mixed ZAJEX-plus-traditional-carrier strategy may be more balanced.

You can review [ZAJEX services and merchant registration](https://zajex.co/en) on the official website, then compare the actual quote against your store's own data before committing.
