Start with the question that brought the reader to the page, then answer it directly before adding depth.

## A structure that is easy to follow

Use a precise title, a short introduction and headings that describe their sections. Cite reliable sources for verifiable claims.

## Write for people first

Do not repeat keywords without purpose. Quality comes from complete answers, first-hand insight and verifiable information.
