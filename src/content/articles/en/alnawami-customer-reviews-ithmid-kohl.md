One customer comment cannot answer a search for **Alnawami customer reviews** or settle whether its ithmid kohl is worth considering. A repeated pattern across thousands of orders is much more useful. We therefore analysed an export containing **11,405 customer-review and question records**, separating star ratings from unrated questions.

The headline result is clear. Among **5,164 star ratings**, the average was **4.52 out of 5**. A total of **4,488 ratings**, or **86.9%**, awarded four or five stars. For ithmid kohl products specifically, the average rose to **4.58 out of 5** across **1,249 ratings**.

## How this review analysis was prepared

We did not rely only on testimonials shown on the homepage. We examined a full Alnawami review export dated **25 October 2026**, containing four record types:

- **2,285 product ratings**.
- **1,504 store ratings**.
- **1,375 delivery ratings**.
- **6,241 questions** without star scores, excluded from rating averages.

This article deliberately highlights **the strongest positive customer experiences**. Customer names, duplicate wording and low-information filler were removed. This selection should not be read as a claim that every review is positive, and the meaning of the quoted comments has not been altered.

> “I bought it in 2024 and returned to buy it again in 2026. The quality is excellent.”

Repeat purchases are more informative than praise immediately after opening a parcel. They show that a customer had time to know the product and still chose to buy it again or share it with family.

## How to read ithmid kohl reviews well

### Prefer detail over generic praise

“Excellent” is positive but limited. A useful review names the variety, colour, period of use, repeat order or packaging experience.

### Separate shopping quality from medical claims

A customer review can provide practical evidence about ordering, packaging, texture, appearance and personal satisfaction. It cannot establish that ithmid treats a medical condition or improves eyesight for everyone. Health-related statements remain **personal experiences**, not clinical advice.

### Confirm the exact ithmid product

Do not rely only on package images. Check whether the product is identified as Isfahani or Moroccan, its colour, weight and included accessories. Alnawami lists several similar-looking formats with different details.

### Read the newest reviews on the product page

The store-wide average is helpful, but a purchase decision concerns one product. Open that listing, read its recent feedback and check its current purchase count before paying.
